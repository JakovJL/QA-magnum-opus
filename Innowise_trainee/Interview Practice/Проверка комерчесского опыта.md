# Проверка комерчесского опыта

### 1. Projects and Deadlines

- Tell me about your most recent commercial project. What was the business goal and what was your specific role?
- Can you describe a situation when the deadline was very strict? How did your team manage to deliver on time?
- How frequently did your team release new versions, and what was your role in the release process?
- Did you ever have to cut scope or re-prioritize tasks due to business pressure? How did you handle it?

### 2. Ownership and Responsibility

- Which part of the product or system were you primarily responsible for?
- Were there features or modules where you were the single point of knowledge? How did you handle that responsibility?
- Can you share an example of a task where your work directly impacted business KPIs or customer satisfaction?
- Tell me about the most complex bug or failure you handled in production.

### 3. Collaboration with Clients and Stakeholders

- How did you typically communicate with product managers or clients about requirements?
- Was there a situation where you disagreed with requirements? How did you resolve it?
- Did you ever face a situation where a client was not satisfied with your team’s work? How was it resolved?
- Have you ever had to explain technical limitations or risks to a non-technical stakeholder?

### 4. Support and Maintenance

- Did you have experience working with legacy systems? How did you approach improvements or refactoring?
- What’s the most challenging production incident you worked on? How was it diagnosed and fixed?
- Did you participate in creating documentation, test cases, or knowledge transfer sessions for future teams?
- Have you ever had to support multiple versions of the same product simultaneously?

### 5. Scale and Domain Context

- What was the approximate scale of the product (number of users, transactions, regions)?
- How did the business domain (e.g., fintech, e-commerce, healthcare) affect your testing or development strategy?
- Did you have to deal with compliance requirements (GDPR, PCI DSS, HIPAA, etc.) in your work?
- How did you ensure system performance and reliability in a high-load environment?

### 6. Growth and Development

- How did your role evolve during the project lifecycle?
- Did you mentor or onboard new team members? How did you organize it?
- What practices or improvements did you introduce in the team that remained in place after you left?
- Were you ever promoted or given more responsibility during a project?

### 7. Handling Change and Uncertainty

- How did you react when project priorities suddenly changed?
- Tell me about a time when business needs forced you to change your testing/development approach.
- Have you worked on projects where requirements were unclear or constantly changing? How did you adapt?
- Was there ever a situation where the project was canceled or pivoted? What did you take away from that?

### 8. Real Business Impact

- Can you share an example where your contribution saved time or money for the company?
- Did you ever identify a critical issue before release that prevented major business losses?
- How did your work improve user satisfaction or reduce support tickets?
- Were you involved in customer demos, pilot programs, or PoCs?

### 9. Cross-Team Collaboration

- Did you work in a multi-team setup (e.g., frontend, backend, QA, DevOps, design)? How did you coordinate?
- Were you part of distributed or international teams? What challenges did you face?
- How did you handle dependencies on other teams when your delivery was blocked?
- Have you ever escalated issues to higher management due to cross-team conflicts?

### 10. Metrics and Results

- Did your team use metrics to measure success (test coverage, release stability, velocity, NPS)?
- Can you recall a situation where KPIs were not met? How did you react?
- How did you personally track the quality or efficiency of your work?
- Have you presented results to management or clients? What kind of metrics did you show?
